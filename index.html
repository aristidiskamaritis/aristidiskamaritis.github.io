FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If your question isn’t answered here, send me a message.

How long does a website take?

Most websites are ready in 1–3 weeks. It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content (text and photos) is ready.

Do I need my own photos and text?

It helps, but it isn’t required. I can help you write the text in English and Greek and source quality images. I can also photograph your business myself with a high-resolution smartphone camera, together with a new logo, in the Photo & logo pack (€110), or recommend a professional photographer.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, because that is where most of your customers will see it.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your website belong to you. There are no lock-in contracts.

What about the domain and hosting?

I’ll help you register your domain and set up fast, secure hosting. Hosting is included in the care plan.

Can customers order and pay on my website?

Yes, with the Online ordering add-on (from €690). Customers order for pickup or delivery and pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or when they collect. Each order arrives on your phone straight away, and there is no commission per order, only Stripe’s card fee. With table ordering (+€150), guests in your café scan a QR code on their table, order and pay from their phone, and can call a waiter or ask for the bill, so you need fewer staff taking orders. You can try both on the Olive & Oat demo.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% when your website goes live. You always get a fixed quote first.
